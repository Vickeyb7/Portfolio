## Brand & Style

This design system targets an executive-tier, technology-forward portfolio and interactive resume for an emerging SaaS Product Manager bridging technical empathy, enterprise sales rigor, and product execution.

### Emotional Disposition & Personality
- **Strategic & Decisive:** Interfaces reflect structural clarity, editorial precision, and outcome-oriented communication.
- **Enterprise-Grade Craft:** Drawing inspiration from high-growth developer-first platforms, high-end fintech, and modern editorial journals.
- **Balanced Authority:** Blends analytical discipline (BrowserStack sales lineage) with visionary product craft (NextLeap fellowship).

### Visual Aesthetic: High-Caliber Modern Editorial with Luminous Accents
The style pairs dark, architectural canvas foundations with disciplined editorial spacing, low-contrast ghost borders, and controlled ambient glows. Visual hierarchy is established via typography scale and micro-luminescence rather than heavy skeumorphism or flat minimalism.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, low-strain canvas anchored in obsidian and deep navy, punctuated by an electric technical blue and elevated by subtle champagne-gold accolades.

### Color Archetypes & Intent
- **Canvas & Underlays:** Deep Obsidian (`#06080d`) to Navy Charcoal (`#0a0d14`) serve as the default application state, offering high contrast for technical case studies and editorial copy without harsh pure-black eye strain.
- **Primary Electric Cobalt (`#1d63ff`):** Dedicated to primary calls to action, active timeline progress nodes, interactive state indicators, and key metric emphasis.
- **Secondary Champagne Gold (`#dfb15b`):** Reserved strictly for distinguished badges, awards, fellowship distinctions, and high-impact qualitative outcomes. Never used for mass text.
- **Tertiary Cyan Glow (`#38bdf8`):** Serves as an illumination tint for micro-gradients, active tab highlights, and telemetry/data tags.
- **Off-White Editorial Neutrals:** Pure whites are avoided for large typography. Off-white (`#f1f5f9`) is assigned for primary headers, while muted slate (`#94a3b8`) handles secondary body text and metadata.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric authority and contemporary presence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and structural triggers with the functional neutral cadence of **Inter** for dense product narratives, case study briefs, and metrics.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Cadence
- **Headlines & Display:** Set tightly with negative tracking to convey conviction and senior product ownership. Titles must remain punchy and scannable.
- **Body & Longform:** Inter balances tabular figures, inline bullet points, and multi-paragraph case study walk-throughs with maximum legibility.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Capitalized or tracked-out small labels (`label-sm`) accompany dates, company stamps, metrics tags, and status pills.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive fluid grid establishes order across wide desktop viewports, condensing to 8 columns on tablet devices and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Layout Model
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, max content container width of 1280px, with generous 48px outer margins allowing side navigation, anchored table-of-contents, and floating action panels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px outer margins; case studies and career timelines reflow into single or stacked dual-column patterns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px outer margins; timelines shift from alternating to single vertical rail alignment.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Vertical page rhythm operates on multiples of 16px. Section transitions use 80px to 112px paddings to provide an expansive, unhurried executive feel.
- Component-level spacing relies strictly on tokens `space-xs` through `space-xl` to maintain predictable densities across modal sheets, cards, and data badges.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews conventional heavy drop shadows in favor of a layered dark glass architecture with delicate light interception and perimeter glow accents.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Base Canvas:** Deep navy charcoal (`#0a0d14`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Feed items):** Semi-opaque slate charcoal (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`) combined with a 1px ghost border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Hovered cards, Dropdowns):** Slightly elevated slate (`rgba(26, 36, 56, 0.85)`) complemented by an electric-blue rim accent (`rgba(29, 99, 255, 0.25)`).
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Modals, Overlays):** Translucent obsidian base with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

### Luminous Borders & Glow Accents
Interactive cards leverage linear gradient borders that react on cursor position or focus states. Key performance indicators and featured ship showcases deploy radial corner glows tinted with electric blue (`rgba(29, 99, 255, 0.12)`) and champagne gold (`rgba(223, 177, 91, 0.1)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a disciplined, engineered curvature (Level 2: `roundedness: 2`). Standard elements feature a base radius of 8px (0.5rem), providing a tailored, architectural silhouette that avoids both clinical hardness and informal bubbly geometry.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Tiles):** 8px (0.5rem).
- **Surface Cards & Showcase Containers:** 16px (1rem).
- **Modals & Flyout Drawers:** 24px (1.5rem).
- **Status Pills, Tags, and Pill Badges:** Full-pill curvature (`9999px`) to create clear semantic separation from content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid electric blue background (`#1d63ff`) with high-contrast white text, subtle top-edge inner glow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`), and 8px corner radius. On hover, a soft 12px blue halo blooms beneath the button.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed by a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`), white text, shifting to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` background on hover.
- **Tertiary / Inline:** Sans-serif label paired with a rightward chevron that translates 4px horizontally upon hover.

### Career Timeline & Milestone Rail
- Continuous 2px spine in muted slate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- Milestone nodes feature an outer ring (`16px`) with a central active pip. Enterprise sales positions (BrowserStack) and product programs (NextLeap) are delineated via dual-color accents (blue for execution/sales, champagne-gold for fellowship honors).
- Timeline cards attach via subtle horizontal stems and elevate slightly upon hover.

### Product Case Study Cards
- Constructed with a 16px border radius, nested inside an ambient container. Cards feature a two-part layout: rich qualitative problem statement and architecture on top, quantitative outcome metrics strip along the bottom.
- Hover states trigger a subtle -2px vertical translate and an electric-blue rim glow.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- **Skill / Tech Stack Chips:** Compact height (28px), 9999px radius, dark navy backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), muted typography, and 1px border.
- **Outcome / Metric Badges:** Champagne-tinted background (`rgba(223, 177, 91, 0.08)`), champagne border, with a gold accent glyph or metric count.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed background (`rgba(6, 8, 13, 0.8)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), 8px radius. Active focus shifts the border to solid electric blue with a 3px matching glow ring at 15% opacity.

### Interactive Modal & Drawer System
- Modals present deep-dive PM artifacts: PRDs, metrics dashboards, user journey maps, and live prototypes.
- Structured with a fixed blurred header, an editorial longform body with custom scrollbars, and a pinned bottom-bar containing direct action links (e.g., Live Product Demo, Figma Board, Slide Deck).
